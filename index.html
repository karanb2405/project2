What Makes the Data Foundation AI-Ready?
🏛️

Governed Data Products

AI should consume trusted, curated, business-owned data products — not raw or untransformed source data.

📐

Semantic Consistency

AI responses must use approved business definitions, KPIs, and rules so that answers are consistent and defensible.

🔍

Metadata and Lineage

Every AI answer should be explainable back to its source system, transformation logic, and business rule.

🔒

Secure Access

AI must respect user entitlements, data privacy classifications, and regulatory constraints for client and advisor data.

📄

Structured + Unstructured Data

The foundation must support databases, documents, notes, forms, policies, procedures, and client communications.

🔌

API-First Consumption

Applications and AI agents should consume reusable APIs and data products — not custom point-to-point extracts.

⚖️

Reconciliation and Trust

AI adoption will fail if business users do not trust the migrated data. Reconciliation gates must be built into every cutover.

📊

AI Observability

Track prompts, responses, data sources, model behavior, hallucination risk, usage, and control exceptions across every AI deployment.
